## Technical information

The supplied records contain preliminary figures for some components, including an 18.5 kW motor, a 4 kW dispersing cutter, plate thicknesses and a Φ108 mm shaft. The records do not reliably identify the exact model or component to which every figure applies. These values are not presented as confirmed specifications of the complete station.

A mixing time of approximately 3 minutes also appears in the source material and requires supplier confirmation for the selected material and configuration.

## Working principle and applications in the source records

The source describes a twin-shaft paddle arrangement that throws material through the mixing chamber to create a temporarily weightless mixing action. It also describes high-speed choppers for dispersing light fibres, such as polypropylene or wood fibre, and reducing fibre clumping and material separation.

Applications listed include anti-crack mortar, thermal-insulation mortar, adhesive mortar, plastering mortar, skim-coat / putty powders and floor mortar. The source also mentions concrete admixtures, feed, chemical powders and food flavourings. Actual suitability depends on the material properties and required mixing result.
