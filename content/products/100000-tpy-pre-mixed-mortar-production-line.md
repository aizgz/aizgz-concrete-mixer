## Product description

The supplied record describes an annual 100,000-ton dry-mix mortar mixing plant. The system is composed of an energy-efficient mixing system, raw-material storage, conveying, lifting, batching, pre-mixing / waiting bins, packing, dust collection, level control, alarm, compressed-air and overall control systems.

Crushing, screening and drying systems can be added according to actual project requirements.

Large storage silos are generally used for bulk cement, fly ash, dry sand and finished mortar. Smaller silos are generally used for minor ingredients such as calcium hydroxide powder, rubber powder, polyvinyl alcohol and cellulose ether.

## Main system components and functions described in the source

1. **Raw-material storage and feeding:** Silos store cement, fly ash and other bulk materials. The source describes pneumatic tanker-truck loading, dust collectors on silo tops and pneumatic arch-breaking devices at the cone sections to reduce bridging and support steady screw feeding and batching.
2. **Mixing equipment:** The source identifies an energy-efficient twin-shaft paddle / gravity-free mixer as the main mixer. It describes automatic feeding, pneumatic discharge, limited material build-up on the chamber wall, no dead corners and a stated mixing uniformity of up to 99%. Confirm the test method and the selected mixer model before treating this as a guaranteed figure.
3. **Sand drying system (optional / project-dependent):** Used to dry damp sand. The listed equipment includes a disc feeder, dryer, conveyors, screening equipment, dust collector and elevator.
4. **Automatic batching system:** Described as comprising feeding screw conveyors, hopper weighing scales, discharge devices and a control system. Ingredients are weighed according to the customer's formula.
5. **Finished-product packing:** Finished mortar can be conveyed to a finished-product storage silo or packed into bags. The source says valve-bag packers are commonly used and describes computer weighing and a dust extraction connection.
6. **Environmental dust collection:** Pulse bag filters are described for collecting dust generated during production.
7. **Conveying and lifting:** Screw conveyors are listed for horizontal transfer and bucket elevators for vertical transfer. The conveying system is described as enclosed.
8. **Automatic control:** The source lists an industrial computer / PLC, electrical cabinet, touch screen and computer-based control, with simulated operating display and automatic weighing control.

## Applications and materials

The source lists interface mortar, anti-crack mortar, bonding mortar, inorganic thermal-insulation mortar, rubber powder, fly ash, putty powder, cement and other decorative mortars and powder materials.
