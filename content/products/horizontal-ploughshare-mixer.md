## Product description and construction

The supplied record describes a horizontal ploughshare mixer consisting of a drive assembly, horizontal cylindrical chamber, plough blade shaft, high-speed chopper assembly, discharge valve and liquid-spray device.

- **Drive assembly:** motor, cycloidal pinwheel reducer and coupling transmit the required speed and torque to the mixing shaft.
- **Chamber:** a rolled steel-plate vessel for the material, with openings for feeding, discharge and observation.
- **Plough blade shaft:** plough blades are mounted to the main shaft through blade arms. The source describes a split / clamp-style construction intended to facilitate disassembly.
- **High-speed choppers:** multiple choppers are driven directly by an auxiliary motor and mounted at the side of the chamber. The source describes multi-stage sealing to prevent dust entering the bearings.
- **Discharge valve:** installed at the bottom of the chamber (except for continuous-discharge arrangements); the source describes manual-handle and four-link operation.
- **Liquid-spray assembly:** mounted around the chamber, using agricultural-type spray nozzles to distribute liquid.

## Features and options listed in the source

1. The liquid-spray arrangement is designed to distribute added liquid through the material, with high-speed choppers helping disperse it.
2. The source describes a small clearance between the plough blades and the chamber compared with conventional designs.
3. Pneumatic sealing is described at transmission sealing points.
4. The observation / cleaning opening is described with an interlock and positioning arrangement for operating safety and sealing.
5. A heating or cooling jacket can be added for heating, cooling or reaction-related processes.
6. Pneumatic discharge is listed as an option for simpler operation.
7. The source states that the high-speed choppers can break up agglomerated material.
