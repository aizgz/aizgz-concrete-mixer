## Product description

The supplied record describes a horizontal dry-powder mixer with a horizontal cylindrical body and inner and outer spiral ribbon elements. It is described as operating smoothly, with low noise, reliable quality and a long service life. The source says the mixer is used for dry-mix mortar, tile adhesive, coatings, waterproofing materials, chemicals, ceramics and refractory materials.

The body includes an access door for cleaning. The source also says a screw elevator or bucket elevator can be configured according to the user's requirements.

## Features listed in the source

1. The clearance between the outer ribbon and the inner wall of the chamber can be adjusted; a cleaning door is provided.
2. Fast mixing and high stated mixing uniformity.
3. The source describes suitability for free-flowing materials as well as materials with poorer flowability or greater adhesion.
4. The source mentions liquid addition up to 10%; confirm the applicable material, process and limit before treating this as a technical specification.
5. Long service life, convenient maintenance and a relatively small footprint are claimed in the source.
