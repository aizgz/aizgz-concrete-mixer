## Product description

The supplied record describes a simple / compact production-line configuration consisting mainly of a gravity-free mixer, dry-powder finished-product bin, secondary elevator and a dedicated elevator for the mixer.

The source describes this arrangement as requiring relatively low initial investment and being suitable for an early-stage operation. It also explicitly notes that the line has lower output than a larger automated plant, requires substantial manual labour and can have a dusty working environment.

## Features and figures listed in the source

- Small initial investment; described as suitable for individual or small-scale investors.
- Compact footprint; stated overall height is less than 3 metres, with the source saying an ordinary residential-style building may be used as a plant. Site suitability and local building requirements must be checked independently.
- Simple operation; the source states 2–3 operators.
- Batch quantity: 1 ton per batch.
- Stated daily output: 10–20 tons/day.
- The source's advantages list also calls the output “high”, while the product description says output is low compared with larger lines. This inconsistency is retained for confirmation rather than reconciled.
- Low maintenance frequency is claimed in the source; no maintenance data is supplied.

## Main equipment

- Gravity-free mixer.
- Dry-powder finished-product bin.
- Secondary elevator.
- Mixer-feeding elevator.

## Applicable materials

The source lists masonry mortar, plastering mortar, thermal-insulation mortar, bonding mortar, anti-crack mortar, self-levelling mortar, runway mortar, repair mortar, highway repair mortar, tile grout, joint filler, flexible exterior-wall putty, interior decorative putty, coatings and other materials for mixing.
