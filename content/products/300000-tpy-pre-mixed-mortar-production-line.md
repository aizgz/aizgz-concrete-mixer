## Product description

The supplied record describes an annual 300,000-ton pre-mixed mortar production line. The listed system includes raw-material storage silos, a batching system, a waiting / pre-mixing bin, a 6 m³ main mixer, conveyors, a computer-controlled automatic weighing and packing machine for valve bags, and an electrical control system.

The record says the line is suitable for ordinary dry-mix masonry mortar, plastering mortar, floor mortar, decorative mortar and waterproof mortar. It describes wear-resistant materials for the mixer liner and components, stable operation, uniform mixing, low energy consumption, relatively low investment, a compact footprint and simple operation.

## Main equipment and process described in the source

1. **Raw-material silos:** The source describes storage for cement, fly ash and sand, commonly using 200-ton cement silos, with other sizes selectable by the customer. Cement and fly ash are loaded by tanker truck; sand is lifted by bucket elevator.
2. **Main mixing equipment:** A twin-shaft gravity-free mixer is specified as the main mixer. The source describes uniform mixing, no dead corners, wear-resistant plates, fast feeding, short mixing time and convenient cleaning and maintenance.
3. **Sand drying equipment:** The listed system includes a feeder, bucket elevator, hot-air furnace, dryer, screen, dust collection, cooling, conveying and lifting equipment. A sand-making machine may be selected according to actual conditions.
4. **Automatic batching:** Hopper weighing scales, screw conveyors, discharge devices, sensors and a computer control system are listed. Materials are weighed according to the selected formula.
5. **Packing:** Valve-bag packing is described as commonly used. The source says the packer can be selected according to customer requirements.
6. **Dust collection:** Dust extraction points are described around silo tops, elevator discharge points and the packing machine.
7. **Conveying and lifting:** Screw conveyors are listed for horizontal transport and bucket elevators or electric hoists for vertical transfer.
8. **Automatic control:** PLC industrial control, electrical cabinet, touch screen and computer are listed.

## Applications

Ordinary dry-mix masonry mortar, plastering mortar, floor mortar, decorative mortar, waterproof mortar, interface mortar, anti-crack mortar, bonding mortar and inorganic thermal-insulation mortar.
