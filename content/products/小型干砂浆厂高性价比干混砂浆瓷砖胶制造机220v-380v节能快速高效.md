Valve-bag packaging equipment is offered in different feeding configurations. The figures below are transcribed from separate source records and must be treated as configuration-specific.

## Impeller type

The source records list the following figures for an impeller-type configuration:

- Ambient temperature: -5 to +40 °C
- Air pressure: 0.4–0.6 MPa
- Electrical supply: AC 380 V, three-phase, four-wire
- External dimensions: 1100 × 670 × 1030 mm
- Motor power: 3 kW
- Filling accuracy: ±0.5 kg
- Recorded packing time: approximately 5 seconds for 25 kg and 10 seconds for 50 kg
- Recorded output: approximately 8 t/h

The packing time and output depend on the material and operating configuration. The model and test conditions for these figures have not yet been confirmed.

## Screw type

The source records identify screw feeding as another valve-bag packaging configuration. No separate technical parameter sheet was supplied for this configuration. Impeller-type figures are not applied to the screw type.

## Pneumatic type

The source records list the following figures for a pneumatic configuration:

- Weighing range: 10–50 kg
- Packing speed: 3–6 bags/min
- Metering accuracy: ±0.2%–0.4%
- Air pressure: 0.4–0.8 MPa
- Air consumption: 0.7 m³/min
- Bag-weight pass rate: 99%
- Recorded output per filling spout: 4–5 t/h
- A maximum figure of 12 t/h also appears in the source record and requires confirmation.
- The recorded electrical supply is written as AC 220 V, 50 Hz, three-phase; this wording requires supplier confirmation.

The source description states that compressed air is used to fluidize and feed the material. The figures above are not interchangeable with the impeller-type specifications.

## Additional functions listed in the source records

The general valve-bagging description lists an automatic feeding system, weighing unit, bag-clamping unit, exhaust / return-air system and electrical control unit. Other source-recorded functions include:

- Sensor-based automatic bag clamping, filling, weighing and bag release.
- Fast / slow feed control to improve metering.
- A double-layer, return-air filling spout intended to release air from the bag and reduce dust escape.
- Human-machine controls for parameter adjustment and data storage.
- An enclosed dust-protection structure.
- For the pneumatic configuration specifically: an integrated air-storage unit, coarse / fine feed control, hopper anti-bridging and automatic bag pushing; a belt conveyor can be configured as an auxiliary device.

## Materials listed in the source records

**Impeller type:** fine powders and fine granular materials, including putty powder, gypsum plaster, dry-mix mortar, coloured cement, mineral powders, chemical materials and organic fertilizer.

**Pneumatic type:** dry-mix mortar, cement, putty powder, vitrified microbeads, dried sand and inorganic thermal-insulation mortar.

Suitability depends on the selected feeding configuration and material characteristics.
