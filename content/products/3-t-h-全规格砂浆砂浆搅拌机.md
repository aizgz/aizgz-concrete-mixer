### I. Main Body of Twin-Shaft Weightless Mixer

* Working Principle: Materials are thrown into the air clockwise where two mixing shafts cross-mix them. For lighter materials (such as fiber and wood fiber), the flying cutters disperse them to achieve optimal mixing results. Generally, each batch takes about 3 minutes to mix uniformly.


### II. Secondary Mixing Finished Product Bin

* Advantages: It evenly transports materials to every discharge port, thoroughly resolving caking issues while providing a secondary mixing effect.


### III. Weighing Packaging Machine

* Advantages: Features impeller feeding for simple maintenance, high speed, and high precision. Packing 25 kg per bag takes only 5–6 seconds, with an accuracy error of about $\pm 0.2\text{ kg}$.


### IV. Pulse Dust Collector

* Characteristics: Easy to install and maintain. It recycles and reuses dust by periodically using high pressure to knock dust from filter bags back into the feeding inlet, solving routine dust cleaning issues.


### V. Bucket Elevator

* Characteristics: Utilizes a belt-type lifting design with an 8-layer thick belt. Buckets are made of ABS plastic (lightweight, wear-resistant, high-temperature resistant, and compression-resistant to reduce self-load and prevent scratching the belt). Chain-length optimization prevents slipping issues.


### VI. Pre-Mixing Bin

* Function: Stores raw materials awaiting mixture, allowing the main machine to work continuously and reducing material-feeding and batching time.


### VII. Weighing Hopper

* Characteristics: Features screw feeding with 3 sensors symmetrically suspended around the hopper, ensuring each conveyor accurately delivers the required materials.
