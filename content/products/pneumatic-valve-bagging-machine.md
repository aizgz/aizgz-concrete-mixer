## Product description

This machine is intended for powder and granular materials with relatively high bulk density and good flowability. Compressed air fluidizes the material and supports feeding into the bag.

## Features listed in the supplied record

- Built-in air storage unit.
- Coarse and fine flow control.
- Hopper arch-breaking function.
- Automatic bag pushing.
- Can be paired with a belt conveyor.
