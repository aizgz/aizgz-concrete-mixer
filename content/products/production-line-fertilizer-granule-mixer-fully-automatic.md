A complete dry-mortar production line is configured according to the project's raw materials, required process, target output and site conditions. Equipment drawings and a proposed configuration can be prepared for the buyer's review. Manufacturing is coordinated after the design and specifications have been approved.

## Equipment and process elements listed in the source records

- Raw-material storage bins
- Batching and weighing
- Material conveying and bucket elevators
- Mixing
- Intermediate and finished-product storage
- Valve-bag packaging
- Dust collection
- Compressed-air system
- Electrical control system / PLC

Crushing, screening and drying are listed as optional process equipment.

## Project information recorded in the supplied materials

### 100,000-ton-per-year dry-mortar line

The source records describe a line with raw-material storage, batching, a twin-shaft paddle / gravity-free mixing unit, intermediate storage, finished-product storage, conveying, packaging, dust collection, compressed air and PLC control. Duplicate source entries have been consolidated.

### 300,000-ton-per-year premixed-mortar line

The source records list a 6 m³ main mixer and an output figure of approximately 40–60 tonnes per hour. These figures are reproduced as preliminary project information only. The annual target and hourly output have not been reconciled, so neither figure should be treated as a confirmed production guarantee.

## Configuration

Plant layout, equipment selection, capacity and automation are determined for each project. Final technical specifications are subject to review and confirmation.
