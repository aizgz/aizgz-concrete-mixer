A complete dry-mortar production line is configured according to the project's raw materials, required process, target output and site conditions. Equipment drawings and a proposed configuration can be prepared for the buyer's review. Manufacturing is coordinated after the design and specifications have been approved.

## Equipment and process elements listed in the source records

- **Raw-material storage:** large silos for bulk materials such as cement, fly ash and dry sand; smaller bins for additives such as lime powder, rubber powder, polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) and cellulose ether.
- **Automatic batching and weighing:** screw feeders, weighing hoppers, discharge devices, sensors and a control system are described in the source records.
- **Mixing:** a twin-shaft paddle / gravity-free mixer is listed as the main mixer in the 100,000-ton-per-year line description.
- **Intermediate and finished-product storage:** waiting / buffer bins and finished-product silos.
- **Material conveying:** horizontal screw conveyors and vertical bucket elevators are listed; the source describes enclosed conveying.
- **Packaging:** valve-bag packaging is identified as a commonly used option. Other bagging configurations can be selected for the project.
- **Dust collection:** pulse-bag dust collectors are described, with collection points around silo tops, elevator transfer points and filling stations.
- **Compressed-air system and plant controls:** air supply, level monitoring, alarms and a PLC-based electrical control system.

The source records also describe an optional sand-drying section. Its listed equipment includes a disc feeder, dryer, conveyors, screening equipment, dust collector and bucket elevator. Crushing and screening may also be selected according to the raw materials and process requirements.

## Project information recorded in the supplied materials

### 100,000-ton-per-year dry-mortar line

The source records describe a line with bulk and additive-material storage, batching, a twin-shaft paddle / gravity-free mixing unit, intermediate storage, finished-product storage, conveying, packaging, dust collection, compressed air and PLC control. One source description states a mixing uniformity of up to 99%; the test method, material and operating conditions are not supplied, so this is a source-reported figure rather than a verified performance guarantee. Duplicate source entries have been consolidated.

### 300,000-ton-per-year premixed-mortar line

The source records list a 6 m³ main mixer, a batch quantity of approximately 6–8 tonnes, an output figure of approximately 40–60 tonnes per hour and an operating crew of 5–8 people. These are reproduced as preliminary project information only; actual figures depend on the material, process and operating schedule. The annual target and hourly output have not been reconciled, so neither figure should be treated as a confirmed production guarantee.

## Basic / compact dry-mortar line configuration

The supplied records also describe a simpler, lower-investment line arrangement. It is a **production-line configuration**, not a separate standalone mixer product.

- Main equipment listed: gravity-free mixer, finished dry-material bin, secondary elevator and the mixer's feeding elevator.
- The source describes this option as requiring less initial investment and having lower output, with more manual handling and a dustier working environment than a more automated line.
- The source also records a claimed batch quantity of about 1 tonne, daily output of 10–20 tonnes, a 2–3-person operating crew and an installation height below 3 metres. These figures have not been independently confirmed and should be checked against the selected layout and equipment before being quoted.

## Configuration

Plant layout, equipment selection, capacity and automation are determined for each project. Final technical specifications are subject to review and confirmation.
