## Product description and working principle

The supplied record describes a twin-shaft paddle gravity-free mixing production line. The mixer uses a paddle arrangement; the source explains that material is thrown through the chamber at a critical rotational speed, creating a temporary weightless mixing effect. High-speed choppers are described for dispersing light materials such as polypropylene fibre and wood fibre, helping address fibre clumping and material separation.

The source describes the system as intended for batch production lines and states that the rapid mixing action shortens mixing time.

## Main system components listed in the source

- Bucket elevator.
- Pre-mixing bin.
- Minor-ingredient bins.
- Twin-shaft gravity-free mixer.
- Finished-product bin.
- Packaging machine.
- Dust collector.
- Electrical control cabinet.
- Gas-phase balancing system.

The source also describes secondary mixing / finished-product storage, a weighing hopper and screw-feeding equipment. Their inclusion depends on the final plant arrangement.

## Production and operating figures stated in the source

- Approximate output: 3–5 t/h.
- Operating personnel: 3–6 people.
- Approximate mixing time: around 3 minutes; the source text around this figure is partly corrupted, so confirm the actual time for the material and selected mixer.

These are source-recorded figures, not guaranteed performance values.

## Other equipment details recorded in the source

- **Secondary mixing / finished-product bin:** one listed configuration has a 7.5 kW motor, 3 mm chamber plate, 6 mm end plate and 8 mm mixing blades.
- **Packaging machine:** the system record lists a 3 kW motor and approximately 5–6 seconds per 25 kg bag, with a stated error of about 0.2 kg. This is a system-level reference and must not be substituted for the specifications of a separate valve-bagging machine.
- **Dust collector:** the source mentions a pulse dust collector with 12 filter bags and a 3 kW fan.
- **Weighing hopper:** a 3 mm steel plate and a 5.5 kW, six-pole motor are recorded; the source describes three sensors supporting the hopper and screw feeding for metered ingredient delivery.
- **Bucket elevator:** the source mentions a 4 kW motor and 3 mm steel plate. Other elevator dimensions and figures are corrupted and are not reproduced.
- **Screw feeder:** the source describes horizontal, inclined and combined arrangements for conveying bulk materials between storage bins, mixers and batching equipment.

## Features and applications listed in the source

The source claims good dispersion and describes the mixer as addressing segregation caused by differences in material bulk density and secondary fibre agglomeration. Applications listed include anti-crack mortar, thermal-insulation mortar, bonding mortar, plastering mortar, skim-coat / putty powder and floor mortar. It also mentions concrete admixtures, feed, chemical powders and food flavourings. Actual suitability depends on material properties and the required process.
