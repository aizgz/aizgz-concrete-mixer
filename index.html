Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +

Our MOQ is 1 unit. We support sample orders for quality testing before full container orders.

What is the standard delivery / lead time? +

Standard models are usually dispatched within 7–12 working days after deposit confirmation. Custom power specs or colors may require 15 days.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept T/T (Bank Transfer), Irrevocable L/C at sight, and Western Union for smaller sample payments.

How do you handle warranty and spare parts? +

All machines come with a 12-month quality warranty on core components. Quick-wear spare parts are available on demand with express global shipping.
